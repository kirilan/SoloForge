# Solo Forge - Creative Brief

## The Product

Solo Forge is a local-first Android fitness app for people who want a simple, private way to track intermittent fasting, calorie intake, weight, and workouts on a single device. No account. No cloud. No tracking. The user supplies their own AI key when they want photo-based calorie estimation; everything else lives in their pocket.

## What The Brand Should Say

- **Self-reliant**: this is your tool. No tribe to join, no algorithmic guilt. You and your effort.
- **Built, not bought**: a forge metaphor - calm, steady, deliberate. Not gym-bro. Not wellness fluff.
- **Quiet**: the app does its job and gets out of the way. The brand should feel the same.
- **Honest**: clean lines, real numbers, no aspirational lifestyle photography.

## What To Avoid

- Hyper-masculine "grindset" iconography: skulls, flames, barbells with motion lines.
- Pastel wellness cliches: yoga silhouettes, leaves, glowing auras.
- Neon gradients, cyberpunk, or glassmorphism for its own sake.
- Stock-photo people. The app never shows other people; the brand should not either.

## Visual Direction

- **Palette**: iron green as the anchor, with a muted ember accent. The product should feel grounded and serious without becoming dark or aggressive.
- **Type**: quiet authority. System fonts are acceptable for now; if custom type is added later, use a neutral grotesque such as Inter, Geist, or Sohne.
- **Iconography**: geometric, single-weight strokes, simple enough to read at notification size.
- **Motion**: minimal. The brand should read as confident and still, not animated or bouncy.

## Required Deliverables

### App Icons

- Adaptive icon foreground + background as separate vector/SVG, designed within the Android 108dp adaptive icon canvas.
- Monochrome icon variant for Android 13+ themed icons.
- Legacy launcher PNGs at mdpi, hdpi, xhdpi, xxhdpi, xxxhdpi.
- Notification icon: pure white silhouette on transparent, 24dp, readable at status-bar size.
- Play Store icon: 512 x 512 PNG, 32-bit, no transparency.

### Logo And Wordmark

- Standalone mark.
- Wordmark "Solo Forge".
- Horizontal lockup.
- Stacked lockup.
- Color, black, and white variants.

### Marketing Assets

- Play Store feature graphic: 1024 x 500 PNG.
- Open Graph / Twitter card image: 1200 x 630 PNG.
- Favicon set: 16, 32, 180, 512 PNG + SVG.
- Social avatar: 400 x 400 PNG.

### Brand Guidelines

A one-page PDF or Figma page covering:

- Color tokens.
- Type pairing.
- Icon construction grid.
- Clear-space and minimum-size rules.
- Three "yes" examples and three "no" examples.

## Format And Handoff

- Master files in Figma or Illustrator.
- Exports: SVG for vector logos/icons, PNG @1x/@2x/@3x for raster.
- Commit sources under `brand/`.
- Use lowercase hyphenated filenames, such as `icon-foreground.svg` and `wordmark-light.svg`.

## Out Of Scope

- Play Store screenshots.
- Promo video.
- Print collateral.
- iOS variants.

## Reference Mood

Restrained, attention-respecting products: Bear, Things, early Strava, Streaks. The goal is not to copy their look, but to match the attitude: direct, useful, and calm.
