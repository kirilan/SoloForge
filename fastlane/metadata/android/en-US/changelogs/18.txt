Fasts can now run up to 7 days: pick Custom and set any target from 1 hour to a week. A weekly strip shows the hours fasted each day, and streaks count every day a fast covers. A new, entirely optional body profile (height, age, sex, activity, goal weight) suggests daily calories and macros, shows your BMI with a healthy range, and draws your goal on the weight chart. Skip any field and defaults are used; nothing leaves your phone.
