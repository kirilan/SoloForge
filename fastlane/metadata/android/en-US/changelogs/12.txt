Food analysis is more honest about what it does not know. An estimate now breaks the meal into its components, and when something is genuinely uncertain the app says which thing it is — the portion, the cooking method, hidden oil or sauce — and asks for that detail instead of guessing. A single analysis is now one request rather than a silent chain of up to three, and a stronger model is used only when you tap for it. Live fasting and workout notifications also keep counting correctly when the phone sleeps, and they come back after a reboot.
