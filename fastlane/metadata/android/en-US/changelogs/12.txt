Food analysis is more honest about what it does not know. An estimate now breaks a meal into its components, and when something is genuinely uncertain the app names it — the portion, the cooking method, hidden oil or sauce — and asks for that detail rather than guessing. One analysis is now one request, not a silent chain of up to three, and a stronger model is used only when you tap for it. Live fasting and workout notifications keep counting while the phone sleeps, and return after a reboot.
