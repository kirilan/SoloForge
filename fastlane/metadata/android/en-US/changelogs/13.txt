You can now choose which model analyses your food. Settings offers four tested options — fastest, balanced, open-weight, and most accurate — each labelled with figures from Solo Forge's own test set: typical wait, how often it answers without a follow-up question, average calorie error, and rough cost. An advanced field accepts any OpenRouter model id, clearly marked as untested. The default is unchanged, so leaving Settings alone keeps the app as it was. Accuracy also improved for everyone: a serialisation bug meant the app never sent its temperature setting, and fixing it cut average calorie error by about six percentage points on our test set. The "retry with a stronger model" button now appears only when the model you picked actually has a stronger one above it.
